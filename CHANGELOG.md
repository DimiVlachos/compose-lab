## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-28

Three Halloween components, and demo clips that show every way in and out.

### Added

- **Spider refresh** (`spider`): pull to refresh as a little spider on its silk. Pull a feed down and it lowers itself from under the chips, swaying with your finger, its eyes going wide past the threshold; let go and it spins a ball of silk, the ball opens into the new posts hanging below it, and it climbs back up its thread reeling them to the top. Let go early and it scurries home. The list flings, a cancelled touch never fetches, and the app bar's refresh button runs the same sequence. Tiny steps, a silk whisper and warm notes, with a tick felt at the threshold; a live region says how many posts arrived. Its demo is a neighborhood feed.
- **Grave undo** (`graveundo`): delete with undo as a tiny moonlit graveyard where the snackbar goes. Swipe a task away and its row arcs down and sinks into the soil under a headstone carved with its name; a skeleton hand pushes up holding it, and tapping the hand or the grave flies it back to its place. Left alone, the hand sinks and the task is gone. Haptics and synthesized soil, crumble and knuckle sounds; Delete actions and keys, and the grave as an Undo button for screen readers. Its demo is Maple Street Bakery's list the day before Halloween, and shows every way in and out: an undo at the grave while the hand rises, a second task buried while the first hand is up, an undo by the hand, and a task left until its hand sinks.
- **Jack-o’-lantern theme** (`jackolantern`): a theme switch that is a small carved pumpkin in a farm app's top bar. Tap it and the candle catches and the night theme spreads out of its face in a soft-edged circle of candlelight over the whole screen, with the grin cast faintly across the feed; flick across the flame to blow it out, and the smoke curls off the lid as the night draws back in. Either change turns round mid-flight. Haptics and synthesized match, catch, breath and crackle sounds; a switch for screen readers and keys. Its demo is Maple Hollow Farm's Tonight screen.

### Changed

- **Paint roller accent** (`paintroller`): its demo also taps Evergreen, which the roller paints in two strokes, and undoes it, so the clip shows both ways to pick a paint and still ends on Blue Ridge.
- **French press summary** (`frenchpress`): its demo also lets go of a push early so the words fly home, summarizes with a tap on the press and gives the story back by pulling the knob up, so the clip shows every way in and out.
- **Photocopier duplicate** (`photocopier`): its demo also feeds a second copy back with Undo and pins a list to the top and back, so the clip shows every menu item and both ways to send a copy back.
- **Pigeonhole move** (`pigeonhole`): its demo first carries a message toward the rack and lets go over the list, so it unfolds back into its slot.
- **CRT picture-in-picture** (`crtpip`): its demo also flicks the set off the side to stop playback and brings it back with Undo.
- **Rubber band unsend** (`rubberband`): its demo also plucks the band a little after the second send, so it twangs back and wobbles and the message stays sent.
- **Coin sorter sort** (`coinsorter`): its demo picks every sort, Price: high to low too, and two of them by pressing the chip and sliding onto the option.
- **Decoder ring translate** (`decoderring`): its demo also lets go of a short twist so the letters spring back, and taps the ring both ways.
- **Recording**: a clip's sound is shifted 170 ms instead of 200 to meet its picture, re-measured since clips play as media.
- **Recording**: `scripts/record.py --sound` records the phone's audio output instead of the app's playback capture, which took every sound before its volume: the paint roller's roll played on at full level for up to two seconds after the roller stopped, and quiet clicks were as loud as the rest. Sound is now shifted 50 ms to meet its picture instead of 170, re-measured for the output.
- **Recording**: `scripts/record.py --sound` refuses a recording whose opening hold isn't silent. scrcpy records everything the phone plays, and the app plays nothing then, so any sound there means something else was playing.
- **Recording**: a demo script may run up to 40 s instead of 26, so a clip can show every way in and out of its component, and `scripts/record.py` waits two minutes for a demo to finish, since it plays the script twice.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-28

Twenty-four new components, sound, a light theme and landscape.

### Added

- **French press summary** (`frenchpress`): a Summarize button on a news story that is a little glass French press. Push its plunger and the story's own words lift off the page and pour into the spout, swirling as grounds; past halfway the mesh presses them down, the coffee clears to amber and three points pour out letter by letter into a summary card. Let go early and the words fly home; tap Full article, tap the press or pull the knob up and every word flies back to its place. The words are real laid-out text. Haptics and synthesized pours, glugs, a tink and the plunger's hiss; a toggle button for screen readers and keys. Its demo is a city hall story from The Harlow Courier.
- **Sound switch** (`core/platform/SoundSetting`, `LocalSoundOn`): the app starts silent. A speaker in home's bar turns sound on app-wide and is remembered across launches; each demo's bar has a speaker of its own that starts from it and changes that screen only. Recordings always have sound. The tour's note under a demo's title is now "Touch to take over", short enough not to be cut off.
- **Paper bag cart** (`paperbag`): a cart icon that is a small kraft paper bag. Products hop into it on an arc. It squashes and rocks as each lands and bulges as it fills, long things poke out of the top, and its count is rewritten in grease pencil. Tap it and it tips open over a list of what is in it, where − lifts a thing back out and Check out folds its top over and places the order. Its demo is a grocery aisle, with haptics and synthesized paper sounds.
- **Star stickers** (`stickers`): a star rating on a teacher's sheet of embossed gold foil stickers. Drag across and each star peels off with a curl, flies up and is pressed into its slot with a glint and a bounce; drag back and they peel off onto the sheet again; a tap jumps to a score. The label names the stars that have landed, the peeled flap is the sticker's paper back, shaded as it bends, and synthesized peel, press and settle sounds and a tick per star come with it. One slider for screen readers and keys. Its demo rates a taco truck order.
- **Mailbox badge** (`mailbox`): an inbox button that is a tin rural mailbox. Mail slips in through its door and the red flag swings up with the unread count painted on it, and comes down once everything is read. Tap it, or pull its door down, to open the inbox. Its demo is a neighborhood app for Elm Street.
- **Padlock password** (`padlock`): a password's strength meter and show/hide switch as a brass padlock. A weak password leaves the shackle swung open and rattling; as it gets stronger the shackle lowers, turns to steel and drops home with a clunk and a glint at strong; tapping the lock swings the keyhole cover aside to show the password. Haptics and synthesized sounds; the lock is a switch for screen readers and the level a live region.
- **Pencil cup FAB** (`pencilcup`): a speed-dial + button that is a cobalt-glazed pencil cup in the corner. Tap it and its four tools (pencil, highlighter, scissors, loupe) leap out in a fan with their labels; tap one, or press the cup and slide onto it, and it flies up and tucks into a pocket at the corner of a new sheet while the rest fall back into the cup under gravity, bounce and rattle with a clink. Done adds the new item, and the tool lifts out of its pocket and arcs back into the cup. Haptics and synthesized sounds; the cup is an expandable button and the labels buttons for screen readers and keys. Its demo is a notes app, Fieldbook.
- **Locket avatar** (`locket`): a profile photo picker as a gold locket on a fine chain. Tap the catch and the lid springs open on its hinge; drag a photo up from Recent and it trims itself to the oval behind the glass; tap or push it shut and it snaps closed with a glint while the chain swings. Grab it to swing it on its chain. The engraving is set in Great Vibes and Cormorant Garamond. Haptics and synthesized sounds; the locket and each photo are buttons for screen readers and keys, and the chosen photo is announced.
- **Birdcage mute** (`birdcage`): a chat's mute switch as a little brass birdcage with a canary inside. Each message makes the canary hop and sing; pull the linen cover down over the cage (past halfway, or with a flick) and the chat is muted, the canary tucks its head and sleeps, and new messages only stir the cloth and are counted; push it up and the canary wakes. A tap toggles it. Haptics and synthesized sounds; the cage is a switch for screen readers and keys, and the state a live region.
- **Zipper scrollbar** (`zipper`): a long list's fast scroller as a brass zipper sewn down its right edge. The list opens behind the pull as you go down, teeth parted over red flannel; holding the pull stands its tab up and swings out a cotton tag with the letter, dragging it scrolls the `LazyColumn` with a rasp, and letting go drops the tab with a clack. A tap on the tape glides the pull there. Haptics and synthesized sounds; a range for screen readers with next and previous letter actions, and keys. Its demo is an address book of 88 invented contacts.
- **Key hook sign-out** (`keyhook`): an account switcher as the walnut key rack by the front door. Whoever is signed in has their keys on a little brass hook under the account card; hang them on the rack and they fly to their own hook, catch with a clack and swing, signing you out; carry someone's set up to the card and let go, and it drops over the card hook's tip into its curl, and you are signed in as them, the old set flying home; the shows below crossfade to the new person's. A carried set stays right under your finger. The keys are pendulums swung by the finger, with haptics and synthesized tinks and clacks; each set is a button for screen readers and keys.
- **Paper fan FAQ** (`paperfan`): an FAQ whose expand arrows are folded paper fans. Tap a question and its fan lifts out of the row and snaps open across the card with a fwap, the answer printed on its pleats; open another and the first folds away before the new one lifts, so only one answer is out at a time; sweep an open fan shut with a finger. Haptics and synthesized paper sounds; each question is an expand/collapse button and the answer is real text for screen readers. Its print is set in Gelasio.
- **Sticky note swipe** (`stickynote`): swipe actions on a list row, where each reminder is a sticky note. Swipe and it peels from the corner like paper over Archive or Snooze, drawn over the app bar as it lifts; pull far enough or flick and it comes off and flies onto a Done or Later pad, or let go early and it presses back flat with a flutter. Tapping a pad puts its top note back, and an empty list says all clear. Haptics and synthesized paper sounds; Archive and Snooze are screen reader actions and arrow keys. Its demo is a Today list of reminders.
- **Paint roller accent** (`paintroller`): an accent colour picker as five little paint trays with a roller. Press a tray and drag up and the roller dips, lifts off full size above the finger and paints wherever it rolls: every accent fill it passes (the route line, chips, goal bar, Start run, switches, the units and links) takes the new colour, glossy and wet, then dries flat. Let go early and it finishes the wall by itself and goes home; tap a tray and it paints in two strokes. The screen is composed twice and the new coat is clipped to the merged rectangles the roller swept. A snackbar offers Undo; haptics and synthesized dip, set and rolling sounds; the trays are radio buttons for screen readers and keys. Its demo is the Appearance settings of a running app.
- **Photocopier duplicate** (`photocopier`): a list's Duplicate as a little office copier. The copier rises from the foot of the screen, the card flies onto the glass face down leaving a dashed outline, the lid drops and the scan's light leaks out of the seams; a warm, toner-flecked copy slides out of the slot and lands right under the original as the list makes room, and the original flips back into its place. While the copier peeks, drag the copy into the slot or tap Undo to feed it back. A real scrolling list with a Compose long-press, haptics and synthesized copier sounds; Duplicate and Pin to top are menu items, screen reader actions and keys. Its demo is a household lists app.
- **Pigeonhole move** (`pigeonhole`): a mail app's Move to… as an oak sorting rack. Pick up a message and the rack rises; as the email nears it, it narrows into a letter page and folds in thirds until only the sender's first name in Caveat and their stamp show, waits above a lit cubby, and slips in behind the rail as the count on the brass plate ticks up and the inbox closes the gap; let go over the list and it unfolds back into its slot. The rack icon raises the rack alone: a tapped cubby's letters lift out and unfold into its folder, Back files them away, and a letter dragged out onto the list goes back where it was. The list still scrolls. Haptics and synthesized paper and oak sounds; a Move to… action, keys and cubby buttons for screen readers. Its demo is Larkspur Mail.
- **CRT picture-in-picture** (`crtpip`): a video's mini player as an old TV. Swipe the playing video down and it switches off: the picture squeezes into a line, the line into a glowing dot, and the dot flies to the corner, where a little walnut set with rabbit ears warms up with the show still playing while the page scrolls under it. Drag the set and it springs into the nearest of four corners; flick it off a side edge to stop playback, with Undo; tap it and the dot flies back into the full player. The set fades as one composited layer. Haptics and synthesized sounds; the set is a button with a Stop playback action and keys. Its demo is a cooking-video app, Skillet.
- **Rubber band unsend** (`rubberband`): for nine seconds after a message lands it stays tied to the text box by a tan rubber band hooked to a brass eye. Pull the band or the bubble and let go, and it yanks the message back down: it bursts into its letters and they drop into the real text field, cursor at the end, to fix. A short pull twangs back and wobbles; left alone the band thins, necks and snaps, and the message says Delivered. The composer is a `BasicTextField` the soft keyboard types into, with Send on the keyboard, and the screen pads for the keyboard. Haptics and synthesized sounds; the caption is an "Unsend message" button and the moments are announced. Its demo is a chat with Jess Parker.
- **Coin sorter sort** (`coinsorter`): a results list whose sort control is a chrome coin-sorting tray. Pick a sort from the chip's menu, or press the chip and slide onto one, and each row tips up into a coin sized by its rank in the new sort, rattles into the funnel, rolls down the rail and drops through the first slot it fits, then falls out of its tube and opens back into a row on its new line. Haptics and synthesized clinks, rattles and ticks; the chip is a dropdown of radio items for screen readers and keys, and the list order is committed when the last row lands. Its demo is a camping store's lantern results.
- **Decoder ring translate** (`decoderring`): a message's Translate button as a brass cereal-box decoder ring. Twist it and the message's own letters lift and spin through the alphabet in a wave across the bubble and click down into English, re-flowing onto the English's lines, measured with `TextMeasurer`; let go past about a quarter turn and it finishes, sooner and it springs back; twist back or tap to see the original. Ratchet ticks, settling clicks and a chime, with haptics; the ring is a button for screen readers and keys and a live region says which language landed. Its demo is a neighborhood chat.
- **Typewriter composer** (`typewriter`): a chat composer that is a 1950s portable typewriter, seen a little from above, with a type basket, ribbon spools, a rubber platen and a bell you can see working, typed on its own tiered, chrome-ringed keys (QWERTY, Shift, Delete, New line, a page of figures and stops) in place of the phone's keyboard, or on a hardware keyboard. Each letter strikes the sheet, the bell rings five letters before the margin and the carriage returns by itself, or by pushing the chrome return lever with a finger, deleted letters are lifted off with correction tape, and the sheet is sent by pulling it out of the roller (or with Ctrl/⌘+Return, or a screen reader's Send action), flying into the thread as a paper slip. Haptics on every strike for any caller; its synthesized sounds are the lab's internal `SoundPlayer` hook. Every key is a named button for a screen reader, and what is typed survives the screen being made again.
- **Smoke delete** (`smoke`): swipe a list row away and it burns from its edge, right under the finger, into smoke that curls up over the list while the list closes under it; draw the finger back and the smoke flows back down into the row as you go; let go before a third has burnt and the smoke is drawn back in, and Undo breathes it back. A row can go the other way in mist. Its demo is a chat list with Clear read, an archive to restore from, and an Undo snackbar.
- **Sound** (`core/platform/SoundPlayer`): demos can play short clips, synthesized by `scripts/synth-sounds.py` (no recordings, so no licences), through SoundPool on Android and AVAudioEngine on iOS, where a clip played faster or slower also moves in pitch, as on Android. On Android they play as media, at the media volume, whatever the ringer is set to; on iOS the ring/silent switch mutes them. A loop starts once its clip has loaded.
- **Pop-up book** (`popup`): a pop-up book whose paper scenery stands up in perspective as a spread opens, with soft shadows and pull tabs that move its pieces; its demo is a Cyclades travel onboarding. The page-turn book's projection moved into a shared `perspective/Homography`.
- **Fishing refresh** (`fishing`): pull-to-refresh where refreshing is fishing: the pull bends a rod, release casts a bobber into a band of water where it floats while the refresh loads, and the outcome plays out on the water, a catch hauled up from the deep on the line into the list, an empty hook, or a snapped line. Built on Material's `pullToRefresh`; the caller hoists a sealed `FishingStatus`.
- **App shell**: a shadowed top bar on every screen; search from home's bar that also finds the versions inside a folder; tag chips to filter the catalog; an adaptive grid of cards, each with an icon and a one-line description.
- **Light theme**: a light palette beside the dark one, and a System/Light/Dark button on home that is remembered across launches. The system bars and the window follow the theme. Each demo's surroundings follow it too, while its art stays the same in both. Recordings always use the dark theme.
- **Landscape**: every demo works on a phone on its side. Layouts branch on the stage's shape (`isWideStage`), and demos keep their controls clear of the sides and foot with `demoInsets`, so recorded clips keep their portrait layout.
- **Magnet filter** (`magnet`): drag horseshoe tag magnets over a table of photos to filter them; a magnet finds every match on the table, pulled in by how well it matches, strong ones stick, two snapped together search for both, and a magnet's photos fan out into a grid where each one opens.

### Changed

- **Recording**: a demo can ask for a tall stage (`tall = true`), recorded as a 9:16 clip with `scripts/record.py --tall`. The fishing refresh's feed uses it, so its clip shows more of the list.
- **Pull cord** (`pullcord`): its Verlet rope moved into `physics/` as `VerletRope`, shared with the fishing refresh's line.

### Fixed

- **Demo screen**: the stage no longer pads the top that the bar already covers, which left a doubled gap over the pop-up book.
- **Pull cord** (`pullcord`): a scripted pull that started as the last one's fingertip faded out was cancelled by it.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-06

The first release: five components, the apps that demo them, and Moodboard.

### Added

- **Nav bar** (`navbar`): a bottom bar built from switchable layers, with a morphing indicator, animated icons, collapse on scroll, a moving cutout and a jelly action button.
- **Profile gallery** (`gallery`, `imagemorph`, `profile`): a profile screen where every open is a shared-element morph, serialised by a morph gate, with a collapsing header and morphing search.
- **Fogged mirror** (`fog`): a steamed-up mirror you wipe with a finger, with running drops and mist that returns; on Android, a camera version that cuts you out of your room with ML Kit selfie segmentation, on the phone.
- **Page-turn book** (`pageturn`): pages that curl in 3D under the finger, drawn in plain `DrawScope`.
- **Paper plane** (`paperplane`): a chat that sends each message as a folded paper dart.
- **Moodboard** (`moodboard/`): a Kotlin Multiplatform app that shares data, logic and ViewModels, with a SwiftUI UI on iOS 26 and a Material 3 Expressive UI on Android.
- Scripted demos for every component, and tools to record them as clips and GIFs.
